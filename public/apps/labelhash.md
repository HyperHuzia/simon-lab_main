# LabelHash

LabelHash is a free Simon Lab tool for engineers: ISO 20560-compliant labels for pipelines, tanks, reactors and vessels.

## Canonical page
- https://simon-lab.org/apps/labelhash/ (Polish: https://simon-lab.org/pl/apps/labelhash/, German: https://simon-lab.org/de/apps/labelhash/)

## What it does
- Generates labels for process equipment and pipelines
- Follows ISO 20560 layouts
- Includes hazard warnings and GHS pictograms
- Applies colour bands for media identification and flow arrows
- Provides a live preview and print-ready export
