# Simon Lab engineering services (summary)

Summary of https://simon-lab.org/services/ (Polish: https://simon-lab.org/pl/services/, German: https://simon-lab.org/de/services/).

Simon Lab takes on engineering orders for chemical plants: a whole project or one discipline of it. The work runs on the Simon Lab platform as project as code, so the documents come out of one model quickly, and experienced engineers check every deliverable before it goes to the client. The page's slogan: "Make your plant AI-native, fast."

## Disciplines
| Discipline | What it covers | Application on the platform |
|---|---|---|
| Process engineering and process automation | P&ID, dynamic process simulation and control programs, tested on an emulated PLC before anything is bought | ProcessView (layer 1): https://simon-lab.org/apps/processview/ |
| Mechanical design | Machines and vessels from dedicated calculations: mixing, shafts, strength calculations (FEM) and flow simulations (CFD), with drawings and reports from the same model | MechGraph (layer 2): https://simon-lab.org/apps/mechgraph/ |
| Automation and power | Control cabinets and their power supply, built from manufacturers' catalogue components, with heat-loss data and engineering registers | IonGrid (layer 2): https://simon-lab.org/apps/iongrid/ |
| 3D modeling | The unit assembled in 3D: piping isometrics, support structures, service access and clash detection | PlantMesh (layer 3): https://simon-lab.org/apps/plantmesh/ |
| Architecture and BIM | The unit in its building and on its site: architectural arrangement and georeferenced OpenBIM models, up to the site plan | Architecture (layer 4): https://simon-lab.org/apps/architecture/ |

## Why it is fast
The project is defined once, as structured and versioned data. Drawings, lists and calculations are generated from that model, so a change reaches every document in the same revision. After every iteration the model is checked against physics, limits and geometry, the way software is tested; AI agents take over the coordination and the repetitive work between the documents. More: https://simon-lab.org/knowledge/project-as-code/

## Who checks it
Engineers make the decisions: the scope, the process, the selection of every machine and component. Every deliverable is checked by an experienced engineer before it goes to the client.

## What the client receives
- drawings and reports as PDF
- models as IFC and 3D Tiles
- lists and registers as XLSX and CSV
- the project itself as structured data, ready for the next revision and for AI agents

## Orders
By email, with no forms: the contact details are at https://simon-lab.org/#contact. A short description is enough to start: the plant or the part of it, the disciplines and the deadline.
