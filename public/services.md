# Simon Lab engineering services (summary)

Summary of https://simon-lab.org/services/ (Polish: https://simon-lab.org/pl/services/, German: https://simon-lab.org/de/services/).

Simon Lab takes on engineering orders for chemical plants: a whole project or one discipline of it. The work runs on the Simon Lab platform as project as code, so the documents come out of one model quickly, and experienced engineers check every deliverable before it goes to the client. The page leads with chemical and process plant design.

## Project scopes
- Process plant design: https://simon-lab.org/services/process-engineering/ (Polish: https://simon-lab.org/pl/services/instalacje-procesowe/, German: https://simon-lab.org/de/services/verfahrenstechnik/). P&ID, mass and energy balances, equipment selection, hydraulics, batch schedules, utility requirements and HAZOP. Deliverables include a 3D model, detailed engineering documentation and isometric drawings of piping and cable routes.
- Power and control cabinets: https://simon-lab.org/services/automation/ (Polish: https://simon-lab.org/pl/services/automatyka/, German: https://simon-lab.org/de/services/automatisierung/). Catalogue components, instrumentation, diagrams and schedules, PLC programming and computer applications for process supervision and management, including applications independent of a PLC. Deliverables include a 3D model and cabinet fabrication documentation.
- Tanks and mixing equipment: https://simon-lab.org/services/tanks-mixing/ (Polish: https://simon-lab.org/pl/services/zbiorniki-mieszalniki/, German: https://simon-lab.org/de/services/behaelter-ruehrwerke/). Equipment selection, working liquid level definition, agitator and drive selection, speed and turbulence selection, impeller geometry, shaft strength calculations and mechanical and structural design. Deliverables include calculation reports, a 3D model of the mixer or complete process unit, fabrication documentation and a procurement list of valves, fittings and instrumentation.

Each page states the scope and deliverables and offers professional support throughout the investment project. Engineers check the documents before delivery.

## Why it is fast
The project is defined once, as structured and versioned data. Drawings, lists and calculations are generated from that model, so a change reaches every document in the same revision. After every iteration the model is checked against physics, limits and geometry, the way software is tested; AI agents take over the coordination and the repetitive work between the documents. More: https://simon-lab.org/knowledge/project-as-code/

## Who checks it
Checked by experienced engineers. 15 years of experience in chemical engineering. Completed projects worth more than PLN 25 million.
